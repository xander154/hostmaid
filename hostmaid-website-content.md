## How HostMaid works

**Eyebrow:** How HostMaid works

## Six steps between "assigned" and "done"

HostMaid does more than schedule a clean. It puts your standard into the job, checks the evidence, and keeps the turnover open until the work has made it through the process.

### 01. Your standard, in their hands

Build a checklist for each property, room by room.

The right number of towels. The owner's closet code. The coffee station. The kayaks that need to go back in the garage. Property quirks stop living in one person's head.

*[Visual: screenshot placeholder, property setup checklist, room by room]*

### 02. Photos it can't fake

The app randomly chooses which checklist items need a photo, and it chooses at the time.

The cleaner takes the photo in the app as they work through the property. Nobody's uploading last week's kitchen.

*[Visual: real screenshot, cleaner app, required photo on a checklist item]*

### 03. The job gets picked up

If a cleaner leaves a property half done, HostMaid offers the remaining work straight away to your certified, company-approved cleaners, with the pay showing on screen. These are cleaners who have already proved they can hold your standard.

**The system chases the work, not you.**

*[Visual: real screenshot, callback offer on a cleaner's phone, with pay showing]*

### 04. Checked before it counts

Finishing the checklist doesn't close the job.

Someone reviews the work first. If something was missed, it goes back while there's still time to correct it.

*[Visual: real screenshot, quality controller review of a room, before the job closes]*

### 05. Receipts for every room

Every clean is dated and documented.

When an owner, a client or a guest asks what happened, you've got a room-by-room record.

*[Visual: screenshot placeholder, dated, room-by-room turnover record]*

### 06. Done means done

The checklist is complete. The required photos are there. The work has been checked. Anything that was missed has been dealt with.

**Now the turnover's done.**

## FAQ

**Eyebrow:** Frequently asked questions

## Before you put another app in your cleaners' hands.

**Will my cleaners actually use it?**
They use it once they see how they get paid. Every job shows what it pays before a cleaner accepts it, and the app tracks what they've earned by week and by month. Pay is set against the property rather than the hour, so a cleaner who works through it properly earns more per hour than they did before.
The record protects them too. When a client says a clean wasn't done, the photos are already there, dated, and nobody's deducting a refund from work that was done right.

**Do cleaners need a smartphone?**
Yes. Photos get taken inside the app while they're at the property, not uploaded from a camera roll afterward.

**How much time does it add to a clean?**
It adds time on the first few properties while a cleaner learns the checklist, and a few minutes per turnover after that for the photos. We won't pretend otherwise. Our own cleaners said the same thing when we rolled it out.
What you get back is the drive time, the callbacks and the recleans.

**Will my cleaners think I don't trust them?**
HostMaid isn't there to catch cleaners out. Everyone misses things, including good people.
The checklist makes the standard clear before the job starts, the photos prove the work when it was done properly, and the review gives you a way to fix what was missed without a phone call.

**Do I need a quality controller?**
Somebody has to go and look. Typically the quality controller drives to the property behind the cleaner, checks the work against the checklist, and handles the jobs a cleaner doesn't do: a light bulb, a filter, leaves on the back deck, something broken that needs reporting.
Once a cleaner has proved they hold the standard, you can mark them certified and their cleans stop needing the check.

**Can I use HostMaid with an outside cleaning company?**
Yes. Add their cleaners and quality controllers to your account as users, and they work to your checklists, your standard, on your properties.

**Can my owners or clients see what happened in a property?**
Yes. Owners get their own access, so instead of forwarding photos when somebody asks, you point them at the property and they can see the record for themselves.
